DJ Joshua Reid is a seasoned force behind the decks, with roots that trace back to high school and a career spanning more than 20 years. Known for high-energy sets and a deep love of disco-infused house, he has shared stages with international heavyweights such as The Cube Guys, Hector Fonseca, and DJ Irene, while performing at iconic events including Toronto Pride and Halifax Pride. After a recent relocation from Toronto to Halifax, Joshua Reid has quickly made his mark on the East Coast, becoming a defining sound of the local bear scene. He now regularly commands the dance floor at the Bear Ball, bringing fun, flair, and undeniable groove to every set he plays.
